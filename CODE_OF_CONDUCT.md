# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in netOP welcoming and harassment-free for
everyone. Be respectful, constructive, and attentive to the impact of your
words and actions. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory
language, threats, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's
private information without permission.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to
sayanc@umich.edu. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions and
may temporarily or permanently ban participation when necessary to protect the
community.

This concise policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
